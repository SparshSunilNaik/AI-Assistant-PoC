Talk to your inbox. Hear your day.

Agentic Assistant is an Android app that reads your Gmail, spots meeting requests, books them in Google Calendar with a Meet link, and reads your day back to you, all from a voice command. Try it below: the phone runs the app's own logic, translated line by line, on a made-up inbox.

Agentic Assistant

Made-up account, inbox and calendar. Nothing here connects to Google.

Say something

Type what you'd say to the phone, or pick a phrase. It goes through the app's parseCommand() exactly as recognised speech would.

The microphone uses your browser's speech recognition; in Chrome, audio is sent to Google to be transcribed. It stays off unless you switch it on.

Background monitor

InboxMonitorWorker runs every 15 minutes, checks the 5 newest emails and notifies you about meeting requests. Skip ahead to see it fire.

          Architecture
          
Six layers, all on the phone

          
There's no server in between: the phone signs in with Google, calls the Gmail and Calendar APIs itself, and does every bit of analysis in local Kotlin. Pick a layer to see its files.
